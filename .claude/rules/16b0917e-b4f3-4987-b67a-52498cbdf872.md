<rule_activation id="16b0917e-b4f3-4987-b67a-52498cbdf872" title="Organize Domain Logic Using Domain-Driven Design Bounded Contexts: Business Logic Organized" applies_to="**/*">
These rules are ALWAYS ACTIVE for all files in the codebase. Business logic must be organized into domain-specific bounded contexts under app/Domains/{DomainName} to maintain clear architectural boundaries, reduce coupling, and improve maintainability.
</rule_activation>

### Rules

- **R-DDD-001** MUST: Business logic MUST be organized into domain-specific directories under app/Domains/{DomainName}
- **R-DDD-002** MUST: Service classes MUST be located in Services subdirectories within their respective domains and contain business logic separate from HTTP concerns
- **R-DDD-003** MUST: Web controllers MUST be located in Web/Controllers subdirectories within their respective domains
- **R-DDD-004** MUST: Controllers MUST be thin adapters that validate input, call the appropriate service, and format the response
- **R-DDD-005** SHOULD: Service classes SHOULD follow a command pattern with a single public execute() or handle() method that encapsulates the business operation
- **R-DDD-006** SHOULD: Use dependency injection to provide services with their dependencies (repositories, external services) rather than instantiating them directly
- **R-DDD-007** SHOULD: Use domain events to communicate between bounded contexts rather than direct service-to-service calls
- **R-DDD-008** MAY: Extract truly shared concerns to a Shared or Common namespace when logic is needed across multiple domains

### Verify

```bash
# Verify all controllers are in Web/Controllers subdirectories
find app/Domains -type f -name '*Controller.php' | xargs grep -L 'namespace.*\\Web\\Controllers' | wc -l | grep -q '^0$'

# Count service classes in proper locations
find app/Domains -type f -name '*Service.php' -path '*/Services/*' | wc -l

# Verify no controllers exist outside Web/Controllers namespace
grep -r 'class.*Controller' app/Domains --include='*.php' | grep -v 'namespace.*\\Web\\Controllers' | wc -l | grep -q '^0$'
```

**Accept when:**
- All controllers are located in Web/Controllers subdirectories within their respective domains
- Service classes are located in Services subdirectories and contain business logic separate from HTTP concerns
- New features added to the codebase follow the domain structure pattern with at least 95% compliance
- Code review checklist includes verification of proper domain organization
- Domain boundaries are clearly defined and documented
- Cross-domain communication uses domain events or dependency inversion rather than direct coupling

<enforcement>
Claude Code MUST NOT skip or defer verification. All files must comply with domain organization rules before acceptance. Violations must be resolved through refactoring or documented exceptions with expiration dates.
</enforcement>
