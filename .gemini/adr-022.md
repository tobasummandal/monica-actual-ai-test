# Adopt Encrypted Secrets Management in CI/CD Pipeline: Secrets Rotation Policies

## Status
Proposed

## Context
Secrets used in CI/CD pipelines require protection against unauthorized access and compromise. Secrets that remain static indefinitely increase the risk window if they are exposed. Automated rotation policies reduce this risk by ensuring secrets are regularly renewed and old credentials are invalidated.

## Decision
We will implement encrypted secrets management in our CI/CD pipeline with mandatory secrets rotation policies.

## Policies

1. Secrets rotation policies SHOULD be implemented with automated expiration and renewal mechanisms

## Rationale
Automated rotation mechanisms ensure that:
- Secrets are regularly refreshed without manual intervention
- Exposure window is minimized through time-limited credential validity
- Compliance requirements for credential management are met
- Operational overhead is reduced through automation

## Consequences

### Positive
- Reduced risk of long-lived credential compromise
- Automated compliance with security policies
- Minimal manual secret management overhead

### Negative
- Requires integration with secrets management platform
- Initial setup complexity for rotation automation
- Potential service disruption if rotation fails

## Implementation
- Use platform-native rotation capabilities (e.g., AWS Secrets Manager, HashiCorp Vault)
- Define rotation schedules based on secret sensitivity
- Implement monitoring and alerting for rotation failures
- Document rotation procedures and recovery steps
