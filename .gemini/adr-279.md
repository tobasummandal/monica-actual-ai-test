# Adopt Domain-Driven Service Layer Pattern for Journal Management: Business Logic Journal

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Journal management operations require clear separation of concerns and maintainable business logic organization. A domain-driven service layer pattern provides a structured approach to encapsulating complex business operations while maintaining testability and scalability.

## Decision
We adopt a domain-driven service layer pattern for all journal management business logic, organizing services within a dedicated directory structure that reflects domain boundaries.

## Policies

1. All business logic for journal management operations MUST be encapsulated in dedicated service classes within the app/Domains/Vault/ManageJournals/Services directory

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Clear separation between domain logic and infrastructure concerns
- Improved testability through isolated service classes
- Scalable architecture supporting future domain expansion
- Consistent organization across journal management features

**Negative:**
- Additional abstraction layer may increase initial development time
- Requires discipline to maintain service boundaries

## Implementation Notes
- Service classes should be named descriptively (e.g., JournalCreationService, JournalValidationService)
- Each service should have a single, well-defined responsibility
- Services should be dependency-injected into controllers or other consumers
