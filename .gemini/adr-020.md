# Adopt Encrypted Secrets Management in CI/CD Pipeline: Teams Use Environment

## Status
Accepted

## Context
CI/CD pipelines require access to sensitive credentials such as API keys, database passwords, and deployment tokens. Managing these secrets securely is critical to prevent unauthorized access and data breaches. Teams need a standardized approach to handle secrets across different deployment environments.

## Decision
We adopt encrypted secrets management in our CI/CD pipeline, enabling teams to use environment-specific secret namespaces for isolating credentials across development, staging, and production environments.

## Policies

1. Teams MAY use environment-specific secret namespaces to isolate development, staging, and production credentials

## Consequences

### Positive
- Secrets are encrypted at rest and in transit
- Environment isolation reduces blast radius of credential compromise
- Audit trails track secret access and modifications
- Reduces risk of accidentally exposing production credentials in development

### Negative
- Additional operational overhead for secret rotation and management
- Requires training for teams on secret management practices
- Potential performance impact from encryption/decryption operations

## Implementation Notes
- Use a dedicated secrets management tool (e.g., HashiCorp Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, or similar)
- Enforce least-privilege access policies
- Implement automated secret rotation where applicable
- Document secret naming conventions and access procedures
