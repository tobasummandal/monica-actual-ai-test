# Adopt Encrypted Secrets Management in CI/CD Pipeline: Secrets Scoped Minimum

## Status
Proposed

## Context
CI/CD pipelines require access to sensitive credentials, API keys, and tokens. Overly broad secret access increases the blast radius of compromised credentials and violates the principle of least privilege.

## Decision
Adopt encrypted secrets management with strict scoping policies to limit secret access to the minimum required level.

## Policies

1. Secrets SHOULD be scoped to the minimum required access level (environment, project, or organization)

## Rationale
Minimum scoping reduces security risk by ensuring credentials are only available where needed, limiting exposure in case of compromise, and enforcing least privilege principles across the CI/CD infrastructure.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Reduced blast radius from credential compromise
- Improved compliance with security standards
- Better auditability of secret access patterns
- Enforced least privilege principle

### Negative
- Increased complexity in secret management configuration
- More granular secret definitions required
- Potential operational overhead in maintaining scoped secrets

## Implementation
- Use platform-native secret scoping (GitHub Environments, GitLab Protected Variables, etc.)
- Document secret scope requirements per pipeline
- Audit secret access patterns regularly
- Automate secret rotation at scoped levels
